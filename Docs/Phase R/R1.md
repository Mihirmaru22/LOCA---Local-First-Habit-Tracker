# Phase R · R1 — Study of Existing Products (In Depth)

> **Scope:** A deep study of products that try to understand, model, or improve a person's life. Goal is not a feature list — it's *why* the survivors are opened daily, *how* they earn trust, and what LOCA (Version 2.5) should copy or avoid.
>
> **Method:** Each product is examined against a fixed question set. Every entry ends with a **Lessons for LOCA** block tied to the D1–D4 audit findings.
>
> **Question set (applied to every product):** problem solved · target user · why opened daily · data collected · explicit vs passive · trust mechanism · hallucination avoidance · self‑explanation · first‑run · onboarding · usefulness with little data · usefulness after months · copy · avoid.

## Per‑product deep dives

### Oura
- **Problem / user:** recovery‑aware daily decisions; health‑conscious adults, biohackers, athletes.
- **Why daily:** one number — **Readiness** — that answers "push or rest today?" It is decision‑relevant before you even open the detail.
- **Data:** passive only (body temp, HRV, resting HR, sleep stages). No emotional self‑report burden.
- **Trust / self‑explanation:** the score decomposes into **contributing factors** ("HRV balance," "resting HR," "sleep balance"), each shown **relative to your own baseline**, which the app spends ~2 weeks establishing and *tells you it's doing*. It never claims to know your feelings — only physiological readiness.
- **Hallucination avoidance:** it only interprets what dense physiology supports; it stays silent/low‑confidence during baseline.
- **First‑run / onboarding:** sets expectations ("we need ~2 weeks"), asks minimal profile, then mostly waits — honest about low early value.
- **Copy:** single decision‑relevant artifact; **baseline‑relative** framing; factor decomposition; earning the number with dense data first.
- **Avoid:** the score can read as a verdict on a bad day; mitigated by neutral language.

### WHOOP
- **Problem / user:** balance strain vs recovery; athletes, optimizers.
- **Why daily:** a **recovery %** in the morning and a **strain target** for the day — plus light social/coaching.
- **Data:** passive HR/HRV continuous; explicit "journal" toggles (alcohol, caffeine, etc.) that later produce personalized correlations ("alcohol → −22% recovery for *you*").
- **Trust:** correlations are **your‑data‑only**, shown with sample counts and a monthly "Weekly/Monthly Performance Assessment."
- **Avoid:** guilt/pressure framing on low‑recovery days; can drive anxiety. The lesson is tone, not mechanism.
- **Copy:** the journal‑toggle → personalized correlation loop is the cleanest "explicit input becomes a personal, evidenced insight" pattern in the market — the opposite of LOCA's discarded‑evidence path.

### Apple Health
- **Problem / user:** a private, unified vault; everyone.
- **Why daily:** rings/trends; but mostly a *substrate* other apps write to.
- **Trust — the defining feature:** **every data point shows its source app/device.** You always know a step count came from your iPhone vs a third‑party app. It **refuses to interpret emotion**; it aggregates and trends.
- **Copy (critical for D2):** provenance is not a screen — it's the **default cell format**. This is the direct antidote to LOCA's Sarah/Alex problem (F7).
- **Avoid:** it deliberately doesn't synthesize meaning — LOCA's opportunity is exactly there, *if* it keeps Apple's provenance discipline.

### Apple Journal
- **Problem / user:** lower the activation energy for reflection; iPhone users.
- **Why daily:** **Journaling Suggestions** surface moments (a photo, a workout, a place, a song) as *invitations to write*.
- **Data:** passive signals become **prompts**; the valuable content is the **user's own writing** (explicit).
- **Trust:** the system never asserts how you felt — it hands you a reason to reflect and gets out of the way. Self‑perception theory in product form.
- **Copy (central to D1):** *data → well‑timed prompt → user reflection* is the highest‑value, lowest‑risk loop available to LOCA. It converts LOCA's signal pipeline into value without ever emitting a verdict.
- **Avoid:** minimal analysis — but that restraint is the point.

### Daylio
- **Problem / user:** near‑zero‑friction mood + activity logging; broad.
- **Why daily:** a 5‑second entry and a **streak**; the ritual is the product.
- **Trust:** **makes no AI claims.** Charts are raw counts and simple correlations ("you're happier on days you exercise" = literal frequency).
- **Copy:** capture friction is the enemy; honest raw counts never overclaim.
- **Avoid:** no synthesis, no memory of *why* — ceiling on depth.

### Exist.io
- **Problem / user:** correlate disparate life data (sleep, weather, mood, productivity); data nerds.
- **Why daily/weekly:** a **weekly review** of correlations.
- **Trust — exemplary:** each correlation shows **strength and an explicit "not enough data yet"** state; it uses hedged language and refuses to assert weak links.
- **Copy (central to D3/D7‑trust):** this is the reference implementation of "never exceed the evidence" for a correlation engine — exactly what LOCA's `PatternDetectionEngine` should present but currently hides.
- **Avoid:** correlations can feel abstract/actionless — pair them with a reflective prompt.

### Gyroscope
- **Problem / user:** beautiful unified dashboard; quantified‑self aesthetes.
- **Why daily:** visual wow.
- **Weakness (a warning for LOCA):** dashboards produce high initial delight and **low retention** — a dashboard is not a decision. This is the trap LOCA's Life tab risks.

### Finch
- **Problem / user:** motivate self‑care via a **pet** that grows when you care for yourself; anxious/younger users.
- **Why daily:** *emotional relatedness* — the pet needs you (SDT's relatedness lever).
- **Copy:** an emotional reason to return that isn't a metric.
- **Avoid:** gamification is a *return* mechanism, not an *insight* mechanism — don't confuse the two.

### Rise Sleep
- **Why daily:** one legible metric — **sleep debt** — plus an energy‑curve prediction you can plan around.
- **Copy:** a single transparent model producing one actionable number.
- **Avoid:** over‑simplification; but legibility beats richness for daily use.

### RescueTime / Rewind / Waking Up / Garmin (brief)
- **RescueTime:** transparent categorization → a productivity score; trust from you being able to inspect/relabel categories (**user‑as‑corrector**).
- **Rewind:** value is **retrieval**, not interpretation — "search your life." No claims to hallucinate. Privacy dread is the cost.
- **Waking Up / Stoic / Reflectly:** ritual + prompt; not personalized, but reliably useful because reflection itself is the value.
- **Garmin Body Battery:** a single "battery" number with a factor breakdown — same winning shape as Oura, earned with dense passive data.

### The graveyard (why they died) — the anti‑reference
- **Google Now:** proactive **opaque** cards; users couldn't tell *why* a card appeared or control it → distrust → folded into Assistant/Feed. **Lesson: opacity kills a prediction product.**
- **Human, Moves:** effortless passive capture with **no synthesis and no daily reason to return** → novelty churn. **Lesson: collection without reflection churns.**
- **Jawbone UP, Basis:** **hardware‑dependent**; company economics + device failures (Basis Peak overheating recall). **Lesson: avoid hard dependencies; LOCA's software‑only, local‑first stance is correct here.**

## Reverse Engineering — Recreating Each Product Mentally

> **This is the mental‑model layer.** For the full technical teardown — scoring math, data schemas, state machines, and the "five engines" framework that rebuilds each product as an implementable spec — see **`Docs/Phase R/R1-ReverseEngineering.md`**. That deep doc is the authoritative reconstruction; this section is the quick per‑product mental model.
>
> Not a review — a reconstruction. For each product we rebuild the whole machine: user journey, information architecture, backend assumptions, data model, learning model, trust model, notification strategy, retention loop, onboarding philosophy, and the value curve across **Day 1 → Day 7 → Day 30 → Month 6 → Year 2**.

### Oura (fully reconstructed)
- **User journey:** wake → glance at **Readiness** on the home tab → optional drill into contributors → live day → sleep → overnight passive capture → repeat. The whole loop is anchored to a single morning glance.
- **Information architecture:** three scores (Readiness, Sleep, Activity) as top‑level tabs; each score is a card that expands into contributor rows; trends live one level down. Strict hierarchy: *score → contributors → history*.
- **Backend assumptions:** you wear the ring nightly; physiology is dense and continuous; a personal baseline is discoverable within ~14 nights; deviations from *your* baseline are meaningful.
- **Data model:** per‑night and per‑interval physiological samples (temp, HRV, resting HR, sleep stages) → nightly aggregates → rolling personal baselines per metric → derived scores.
- **Learning model:** rolling baseline estimation per metric (adaptive normal); scores are deviations vs. that baseline. Learning = the baseline slowly tracking you; no emotional inference.
- **Trust model:** every score decomposes into named contributors, each shown relative to your baseline; the app openly states the baseline‑building period and lowers confidence during it. It only claims physiological readiness — never feelings.
- **Notification strategy:** sparse and morning‑anchored ("Readiness ready"); occasional bedtime nudge. Calm.
- **Retention loop:** morning glance → plan the day → behavior → overnight measurement confirms/adjusts → next morning. The measurement *closes the loop on yesterday's choice*.
- **Onboarding philosophy:** honest low‑early‑value; set expectations, ask minimal profile, then wait.
- **Value curve:** **Day 1** — device paired, "collecting." **Day 7** — provisional scores, still stabilizing. **Day 30** — trustworthy baseline, deviations meaningful. **Month 6** — trends, seasonal/cycle patterns. **Year 2** — long‑arc physiology, illness/cycle prediction, deep personalization.

### WHOOP (fully reconstructed)
- **User journey:** morning **Recovery %** → get a **Strain target** → live strain accrues through the day → optional journal toggles at night → weekly/monthly assessment.
- **Information architecture:** Recovery / Strain / Sleep as the spine; the **Journal** is a first‑class input surface; the **Monthly Performance Assessment** is the payoff artifact.
- **Backend assumptions:** continuous HR/HRV; the user will answer a handful of yes/no behavior toggles that become personalized covariates.
- **Data model:** continuous cardiovascular stream → recovery/strain scores → journal booleans joined against recovery over time.
- **Learning model:** per‑user correlation engine — each journal behavior's effect on *your* recovery is estimated as data accrues ("alcohol → −22% for you"), with sample counts.
- **Trust model:** correlations are your‑data‑only, shown with n; baseline‑relative; hedged until enough samples.
- **Notification strategy:** morning recovery, strain progress, sleep‑need reminders; more coaching‑flavored than Oura.
- **Retention loop:** the **journal → personalized correlation** loop is the retention engine — you keep logging behaviors because it pays you back with *your* numbers.
- **Onboarding philosophy:** athlete framing; get the band on, start baseline, introduce the journal early to seed the correlation engine.
- **Value curve:** **D1** collecting. **D7** first recovery trends. **D30** first personalized correlations. **M6** robust behavior↔recovery map. **Y2** longitudinal performance/coaching.

### Apple Health (fully reconstructed)
- **User journey:** mostly *ambient* — other apps write, user occasionally opens to check rings/trends or a specific metric's source.
- **Information architecture:** Summary (favorites/rings) → per‑category detail → **per‑datum source & access** always reachable. Provenance is never more than a tap away.
- **Backend assumptions:** it is a **vault and router**, not an interpreter; many sources write; the user owns and can inspect lineage.
- **Data model:** typed samples with mandatory **source device/app** and timestamp; aggregates and trends computed on top; nothing emotional.
- **Learning model:** essentially none by design — trends, not inference. (Deliberate restraint.)
- **Trust model:** **provenance‑by‑construction** — source on every datum; refuses to interpret meaning it can't source.
- **Notification strategy:** minimal (trends, occasional highlights).
- **Retention loop:** it's infrastructure — retention comes from being the substrate other apps and the watch depend on.
- **Onboarding philosophy:** near‑zero; it accretes as you use the phone/watch.
- **Value curve:** **D1** steps already there. **D7** a week of trends. **D30** monthly trends. **M6** seasonal. **Y2** multi‑year health archive.

### Apple Journal (fully reconstructed)
- **User journey:** open (or get a suggestion notification) → see **Journaling Suggestions** (a photo, workout, place, song) → tap one → *write* → save.
- **Information architecture:** a reverse‑chronological journal + a **Suggestions** shelf that turns passive signals into writing prompts. Signals are *scaffolding*, the entry is the artifact.
- **Backend assumptions:** on‑device signals (photos, location, workouts, music, contacts) can propose *moments worth reflecting on*; the user supplies meaning.
- **Data model:** ephemeral suggestion objects (signal‑derived) → user‑authored entries (the durable data). The system never stores a claim about how you felt.
- **Learning model:** suggestion ranking (which moments to surface) — not state inference. It learns *what to prompt*, not *who you are*.
- **Trust model:** suggestions are invitations, never assertions; the user is the sole author of interpretation. Zero overclaim surface.
- **Notification strategy:** gentle, opt‑in journaling reminders + occasional suggestion nudges.
- **Retention loop:** signal → well‑timed prompt → reflection → a growing personal record you return to. Reflection itself is the reward.
- **Onboarding philosophy:** ask almost nothing; demonstrate value with a first suggestion immediately.
- **Value curve:** **D1** a prompt to write. **D7** a small streak of entries. **D30** a browsable month. **M6** a meaningful record. **Y2** an autobiography you reread — the compounding asset.

### Daylio (fully reconstructed)
- **User journey:** open → tap mood + activity chips → done in ~5s → occasional chart glance.
- **Information architecture:** entry flow front‑and‑center; stats/streak secondary; correlations are literal counts.
- **Backend assumptions:** friction is the enemy; users will log daily only if it's near‑instant; no AI needed.
- **Data model:** discrete mood level + activity tags per entry. That's it.
- **Learning model:** none — frequency counts and simple co‑occurrence ("happier on gym days" = literal average).
- **Trust model:** **no‑claim honesty** — nothing to hallucinate; charts are raw.
- **Notification strategy:** a daily reminder to log (the streak engine).
- **Retention loop:** habit + streak + loss aversion. The ritual is the product.
- **Onboarding philosophy:** get to first entry in seconds.
- **Value curve:** **D1** first entry. **D7** a streak forming. **D30** a mood calendar + first patterns. **M6** seasonal mood shape. **Y2** a long mood diary.

### Exist.io (fully reconstructed)
- **User journey:** connect data sources → live normally → **weekly review** of correlations and trends → optional mood tagging to enrich.
- **Information architecture:** a correlations/insights feed + per‑attribute trend pages; each insight carries strength + data sufficiency.
- **Backend assumptions:** disparate sources (sleep, weather, steps, productivity, mood) can be joined; correlations need a minimum n before they're worth showing.
- **Data model:** daily per‑attribute values aggregated across sources → pairwise correlations over time windows.
- **Learning model:** rolling pairwise correlation with significance/sufficiency gating.
- **Trust model:** **exemplary hedging** — shows correlation strength and an explicit "not enough data yet"; refuses weak links; language is soft.
- **Notification strategy:** weekly review is the beat; light daily reminders to tag mood.
- **Retention loop:** the weekly review is the appointment; correlations compound as data grows.
- **Onboarding philosophy:** connect sources, set expectations that insight is weeks away.
- **Value curve:** **D1** sources connected, nothing yet. **D7** first weekly review, thin. **D30** first real correlations. **M6** a rich correlation map. **Y2** long‑horizon life correlations.

### Finch (fully reconstructed)
- **User journey:** open → your pet greets you → do a self‑care task/check‑in → pet grows/adventures → return tomorrow to care for it.
- **Information architecture:** the **pet** is the home; tasks/check‑ins are the input; growth/collection is the reward layer.
- **Backend assumptions:** *emotional relatedness* (the pet needs you) motivates self‑care better than metrics.
- **Data model:** micro check‑ins, tasks, streaks → pet growth/energy state.
- **Learning model:** essentially none — it's a motivation scaffold, not an inference engine.
- **Trust model:** makes no analytical claims about you, so nothing to distrust.
- **Notification strategy:** warm, character‑voiced nudges ("your Finch is waiting").
- **Retention loop:** care‑for‑the‑pet + relatedness + collection. Strong emotional return hook.
- **Onboarding philosophy:** name your pet, do one tiny task, feel the bond immediately.
- **Value curve:** **D1** a bonded pet + one task. **D7** streak + growth. **D30** habit scaffold established. **M6** sustained self‑care ritual. **Y2** depends on novelty/content freshness (the risk).

### Rise Sleep (compact reconstruction)
- **Journey:** morning **sleep debt** + energy‑curve → plan day around peaks/dips. **IA:** one metric + one predicted curve. **Backend assumption:** sleep debt and circadian phase are enough to guide a day. **Data/learning:** sleep duration history + circadian model → debt + energy prediction. **Trust:** simple transparent model. **Notifications:** melatonin/wind‑down/peak windows. **Retention:** plan‑your‑day utility. **Onboarding:** import sleep, show the curve fast. **Curve:** D1 estimate → D7 personalized → D30 reliable → M6 habit → Y2 (retention pressure once the number stabilizes).

### LOCA reverse‑engineered *as it currently is* (the honest mirror)
- **User journey (today):** log a habit (instant, real) → open Life tab → read a prose "Today's Read" → browse mostly‑empty cards. The loop terminates in prose, not a decision.
- **Information architecture:** Habits tab (real, valuable) + Life tab (Today's Read → Browse row of 12 → Questions). Life leads with narration, not a legible artifact.
- **Backend assumptions (unverified/false):** that habit checkboxes carry enough signal to infer four emotional dimensions; that a personal baseline exists early (it doesn't — placeholder 0.50/0.45/0.50/0.55, F6); that hourly inference over sparse signals is meaningful.
- **Data model:** `LogEntry` (FACT) → `HabitBridgeManager` → `.explicitLog` `SignalEvent` → hourly `InferredState` → composed `PresentScene`. Clean until inference.
- **Learning model:** four weighted models (energy/stress/focus/mood) — but **non‑renormalized weights (F1)** deflate every dimension for sensor‑poor users, **dimension‑blind logged component (F2)** contaminates across dimensions, and **mood‑only patterns (F3)** leave 3/4 dimensions with no surface. The learning is mathematically wrong before the UI.
- **Trust model:** *inverted* — computes `InferenceProvenance` + `UncertaintyCalculus` then **discards them at the UI (F8)**; renders confident prose over deflated numbers vs a placeholder baseline. Seeded entities (Sarah/Alex, F7) are indistinguishable from real. This is the opposite of Apple Health/Oura/Exist.
- **Notification strategy:** habit reminders only ("Time to log"); no Life‑aware beat. No retention appointment like Oura's morning or Exist's weekly review.
- **Retention loop:** effectively none on the Life side — nothing pays the user back for logging, and the read isn't a decision. Exposed to all four graveyard failure modes.
- **Onboarding philosophy:** conceptual 4‑card sheet; asks nothing, seeds no goal, postpones no permission with a named payoff.
- **Value curve (today):** **D1** empty (honest). **D7** deflated/misleading read (F1). **D30** thin patterns if inference fixed. **M6** a real record *if* it survives churn. **Y2** the genuine potential — a truthful life archive — but only if the truthful record is protected and led with.

### What the reconstruction reveals for LOCA (tied to D1–D4)
1. Every survivor's machine terminates in **one legible artifact + a retention appointment**; LOCA's terminates in prose with no appointment. → **D1**.
2. Every interpreter's **learning model is baseline‑relative and factor‑decomposed and earned by dense signal**; LOCA's is non‑renormalized, contaminated, and unearned. → **D4** (F1/F2/F3).
3. Every trusted product's **trust model is provenance/hedging by construction**; LOCA's discards the provenance it computes. → **D2/D3** (F7/F8).
4. The lowest‑risk, highest‑value machine to copy wholesale is **Apple Journal's**: signal → well‑timed prompt → user reflection → compounding record. It requires *no* correct inner‑state inference, so it routes around D4 entirely while directly answering D1.

## Cross‑product synthesis

### What the daily‑opened survivors share
1. **One legible, personal, decision‑relevant artifact** (Readiness / Strain / Sleep Debt / Body Battery / streak / pet).
2. **Baseline‑relative** interpretation, never absolute verdicts.
3. **Factor decomposition** — the number always shows what fed it.
4. **Earned interpretation** — dense passive signal *precedes* any claim; thin‑data periods are explicitly low‑confidence.
5. **Provenance discipline** (Apple Health) or **no‑claim honesty** (Daylio) or **hedged correlations** (Exist).
6. **Reflection over narration** (Apple Journal) — the highest‑value, lowest‑risk loop.

### What the dead share
1. Opaque prediction (Google Now).
2. Capture without synthesis (Human, Moves).
3. Hardware dependence (Jawbone, Basis).
4. Effort > payoff; novelty without compounding.

## Lessons for LOCA (consolidated, tied to D1–D4)

1. **Build one legible daily artifact.** LOCA has none; Today's Read is prose, not a decision. → **D1**. Model it on Readiness/Body Battery: a single object, baseline‑relative, with a factor breakdown — *but only for dimensions LOCA has earned signal for.*
2. **Show the factors you already compute.** `InferenceProvenance` holds `contributingSources` + `sampleCount`; the UI discards them (F8). Every survivor that interprets shows exactly this. → **D3**.
3. **Provenance as default cell format** (Apple Health). Seeded vs inferred vs user vs calendar must be visually distinct — the Sarah/Alex fix. → **D2 / F7**.
4. **Earn the claim or stay silent.** Oura/WHOOP earn scores with dense physiology; LOCA infers four emotional dimensions from **checkboxes** — the signal isn't there, which is why F1 deflation is fatal. Either add earned signal (Health/check‑ins) or don't emit the state number. → **D4**.
5. **Adopt the Apple Journal loop.** *Signal → well‑timed prompt → user reflection.* This converts LOCA's existing pipeline into value without any verdict, and dodges the entire deflation/overclaim risk. → **D1 / D3**.
6. **Copy Exist.io's hedged correlation language** for Patterns (strength + "not enough data yet"), and copy WHOOP's explicit‑input→personal‑correlation loop for the micro‑check‑ins. → **D3 / D4‑F3**.
7. **Copy RescueTime/Rewind's user‑as‑corrector stance** — surface `SensorConflict`/feedback so the user teaches the model visibly. → **D4**.
8. **Heed the graveyard:** LOCA is currently exposed to opacity (F8), capture‑without‑synthesis (empty Life), and effort>payoff (heavy logging, thin return). The churn‑resistant asset is the **truthful long‑term record** — lead with it.
