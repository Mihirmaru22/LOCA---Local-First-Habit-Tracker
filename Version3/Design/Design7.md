# LOCA Version 3 · Design Phase
## Design7 — Content Architecture

> **The single question Design7 answers:** *What does each surface actually contain — what information, in what priority, and why?*
>
> This is **content architecture**, the definitive specification of what every surface holds before a single screen is designed. It contains **no wireframes, no mockups, no Figma, no SwiftUI, no implementation, no visual styling.** Every decision is derived from and consistent with the frozen foundation — Phase R (R1–R10), Phase S (S1–S7), Design1 (product), Design2 (experience), Design3 (information), Design4 (interaction), Design5 (visual philosophy), Design6 (surfaces) — and Version 2's cards are preserved only if they earn it here.
>
> **The governing rule:** *every surface must earn every piece of information it contains, and every surface must get calmer as it deepens — never denser* (Design5). Content that cannot justify its place against the person's understanding (S3) does not appear.

---

## Part I — Content Principles (the rules for any surface)

1. **Every element earns its place.** An element exists only if it serves the person's understanding *on this surface*; otherwise it is removed (Design5 "information before decoration"; S7 Art. IX).
2. **Calmer, not denser.** New evidence and depth are pushed *down* into deeper levels (L2/L3), never crowded into the first glance. A surface that learns more becomes *quieter at the top*, not busier. *(Design5; Design2 deepen‑without‑intensifying.)*
3. **Progressive detail (L1 → L2 → L3).** Every surface reveals in three levels: instantly understandable → after curiosity → after deliberate exploration. *(Design5 progressive disclosure.)*
4. **Evidence‑gated appearance.** Any claim‑bearing element appears only when its evidence exists; below threshold it is absent or replaced by honest "not enough yet." *(Design3/4; S4 abstention.)*
5. **Honesty in every claim‑bearing element.** Provenance and calibrated confidence travel with any observation; facts are shown plainly. *(S6; Design3.)*
6. **One canonical home.** No concept is *owned* by two surfaces; others summarize and link. *(Design6; Design3.)*
7. **The one‑way flow holds.** Content derived from facts never rewrites the facts it displays. *(Design3.)*

## Part II — Surface Content Contracts

Each contract: **Purpose · Questions answered · L1/L2/L3 · Primary/Secondary actions · Belongs · Never · Inputs · Outputs · Dependencies · Evidence & Confidence · Expectation · Failure · Empty · Connections.**

### TODAY (daily home)
- **Purpose:** the daily reflective moment. **Questions:** *How do I reflect on today? What happened today? (maybe) anything worth noticing?*
- **L1:** one calm reflective invitation/prompt + today's context (day). **L2:** today's record (facts, provenance‑tagged); at most **one** earned observation‑as‑question (hedged, confidence shown); an occasional nostalgia teaser. **L3:** the evidence behind an observation; the fuller day.
- **Primary actions:** reflect (write), capture a note, state check‑in, quick‑log a habit. **Secondary:** open Ask, open a Review teaser, correct a fact.
- **Belongs:** one prompt, today's facts, ≤1 earned observation, capture affordances. **Never:** a dashboard, multiple observations, a narrated verdict, streak pressure, a metrics grid.
- **Inputs:** today's record; direction (framing); earned observations (if any). **Outputs:** a saved reflection (→ Timeline), a state check‑in (→ Understanding/calibration), a note. **Dependencies:** the record; the prompt‑seeding of the reflection engine.
- **Evidence/Confidence:** the prompt is not a claim (needs none); any observation requires an L4 pattern + minimum confidence, shown, else absent. **Expectation:** a calm daily moment, honest about having little early. **Failure:** if nothing to seed → a gentle honest generic prompt or just the invitation; never fabricate. **Empty (day 1):** teaching — "a quiet place to reflect; it deepens as you go."
- **Connections:** → Habits, → Ask, → Reviews (teaser), → Timeline (saved reflection & nostalgia).

### HABITS HOME (daily)
- **Purpose:** capture the doing. **Questions:** *What am I tracking? Did I do it? How consistent have I been (neutrally)?*
- **L1:** today's habits + quick‑log. **L2:** gentle, neutral consistency per habit. **L3:** → Habit detail.
- **Primary:** log; add/edit habit; open a habit. **Secondary:** reorder, archive.
- **Belongs:** the habit list, log affordance, neutral consistency‑as‑information. **Never:** streak‑loss drama, guilt, scores, leaderboards, coercion.
- **Inputs:** habit definitions + logs. **Outputs:** a new log. **Dependencies:** none (deterministic). **Evidence/Confidence:** facts only — no inference here. **Expectation:** fast, kind capture. **Failure:** n/a. **Empty:** "add a habit to begin."
- **Connections:** → Habit detail, → Today (quick‑log), → Understanding (habits as evidence).

### HABIT DETAIL (occasional)
- **Purpose:** understand one habit honestly. **Questions:** *What's my history? When do I do it? Does it connect to anything (hedged)?*
- **L1:** the habit + recent history + neutral consistency. **L2:** its hedged L4 patterns (provenance + confidence) *if earned*; a "log your state" calibration affordance. **L3:** the evidence behind a pattern; full history.
- **Primary:** log; view history; ask‑why on a pattern; correct. **Secondary:** edit habit, calibrate.
- **Belongs:** history (fact), consistency (fact), hedged patterns (if earned), calibration entry. **Never:** a fixed trait, a causal claim, a discipline verdict, a chart of inferred data shown as precise.
- **Evidence/Confidence:** a pattern requires L4 + confidence, shown. **Expectation:** honest history + humble patterns. **Failure:** abstain. **Empty pattern:** "patterns will appear as data builds."
- **Connections:** → Understanding (canonical pattern home), → Timeline.

### TIMELINE (occasional)
- **Purpose:** the truthful record over time (memory). **Questions:** *What has my life held? What happened around then? What were the turning points I confirmed?*
- **L1:** recent record + confirmed landmarks as a calm sequence. **L2:** a chosen period; "this time last year." **L3:** a moment's detail + its provenance.
- **Primary:** revisit; confirm/name a chapter or event; reflect on a moment; correct an entry. **Secondary:** jump to a period.
- **Belongs:** facts, confirmed landmarks, saved reflections. **Never:** an AI‑authored narrative overwriting the record; unconfirmed "events" asserted as fact; fabricated landmarks.
- **Inputs:** the record; candidate landmarks (for confirmation). **Outputs:** a confirmed landmark; a correction; a reflection. **Evidence/Confidence:** a landmark *candidate* requires the person's confirmation to become fact. **Expectation:** a faithful, revisitable life‑record. **Failure:** show the record as‑is; never invent. **Empty:** "your record will grow here as you go."
- **Connections:** → Chapter/Event detail, → Today (newest), → Reviews.

### REVIEWS (weekly / monthly)
- **Purpose:** the honest reflective ritual. **Questions:** *What did my week/month hold? Anything worth noticing? What do I make of it?*
- **L1:** a brief honest look‑back (real facts: habits done, notable moments) + one reflective invitation. **L2:** ≤ a couple hedged observations in context (provenance); the person's reflection space. **L3:** evidence; deep‑links to Timeline/Understanding.
- **Primary:** read, reflect, save. **Secondary:** ask‑why, correct.
- **Belongs:** honest rollups (facts), few hedged observations, a reflection prompt. **Never:** a scorecard, a grade, a "you did X% better" judgment, dense inferred charts.
- **Evidence/Confidence:** observations require L4 + confidence; the fact‑rollups are certain. **Expectation:** a fair, honest, unhurried look‑back. **Failure/thin week:** a shorter honest review ("a quiet week"). **Empty:** does not appear until a week of data exists.
- **Connections:** → Timeline, → Understanding, → Today (teaser/notification).

### UNDERSTANDING (occasional)
- **Purpose:** hedged, correctable observations about the person. **Questions:** *What seems true about me, and why? How sure is LOCA? Is it right?*
- **L1:** the current few honest observations (hedged, confidence shown) — or "not enough yet." **L2:** the evidence + provenance behind each; confidence history. **L3:** the raw facts; correction/reject/hide.
- **Primary:** read, ask‑why. **Secondary:** correct, reject, hide.
- **Belongs:** L4 patterns only, hedged, provenance‑borne, correctable. **Never:** verdicts, fixed traits, causation, a dense feed, anything unexplained.
- **Evidence/Confidence:** each observation requires L4 + min confidence; below → not shown. **Expectation:** honest, humble, correctable self‑observations. **Failure:** abstain. **Empty:** teaching — "as you log, honest observations will appear here, and you can always correct them."
- **Connections:** → Observation detail (evidence), → Ask, → the facts behind each claim, → Habit detail (summaries).

### PEOPLE (occasional · consent‑gated)
- **Purpose:** relationships as part of understanding one's own life. **Questions:** *Who recurs in my life? Where did that come from?*
- **L1:** the recurring people, each provenance‑labeled, salience honest. **L2:** → Person detail (context, appearances). **L3:** the evidence (calendar/notes) with provenance.
- **Primary:** view, confirm, ask‑why. **Secondary:** correct, hide.
- **Belongs:** recurring people, provenance, honest salience. **Never:** a CRM, an asserted relationship *nature*, anyone unexplained (the F7 fix), social features.
- **Dependencies/Evidence:** requires consent (e.g. Calendar) + a recurrence threshold; nature is confirmation‑required. **Expectation:** honest, explained, consented. **Failure:** without consent → the payoff‑named empty. **Empty (no consent):** "grant Calendar to see who you spend time with."
- **Connections:** → Person detail, → Timeline (when together), → Understanding.

### DIRECTION (occasional · user‑authored)
- **Purpose:** the person's own values/goals/where‑heading. **Questions:** *Where am I heading? What matters to me? (context) what has this time held?*
- **L1:** the person's direction statement + values + open questions. **L2:** honest trajectory *context* (evidence, never advice). **L3:** the evidence behind that context.
- **Primary:** author, edit. **Secondary:** add/resolve an open question.
- **Belongs:** user‑authored content; hedged trajectory context. **Never:** prescription, advice, judgment on divergence.
- **Evidence/Confidence:** content is user‑authored (stored as fact, no evidence needed); trajectory context requires evidence + is hedged. **Expectation:** the person's own, never told what to do. **Failure:** show only what's authored. **Empty:** "set your direction — optional; it helps LOCA understand your context."
- **Connections:** → Reviews (framing), → Today (context).

### ASK (invoked)
- **Purpose:** self‑inquiry. **Questions:** whatever the person asks of their own life.
- **L1:** the question input + the grounded answer (or honest abstention). **L2:** the evidence/provenance behind the answer; refine. **L3:** the raw facts.
- **Primary:** ask, read, save. **Secondary:** ask‑why, refine.
- **Belongs:** the answer — grounded, hedged, provenance‑bearing; abstention when thin. **Never:** a confident answer beyond evidence; a chatbot stream; answers about others or the world.
- **Inputs:** the question + the record + L1–L4. **Outputs:** an answer; a saved answer (if kept). **Evidence/Confidence:** composed at answer‑time; confidence shown; insufficient → honest abstention. **Expectation:** an honest answer to *my* question, or an honest "not yet." **Failure:** "I don't have enough to answer that yet." **Empty:** a gentle prompt to ask.
- **Connections:** ← Today/Life; → Saved answers; → the facts behind the answer.

### YOU / TRUST — BELIEFS (occasional · essential)
- **Purpose:** inspect + correct what LOCA believes. **Questions:** *What does LOCA think it knows about me? Why? Is it right? Can I change or remove it?*
- **L1:** the current beliefs, each with confidence + provenance. **L2:** the evidence + confidence history behind each. **L3:** correction/removal; reset understanding.
- **Primary:** inspect, correct, remove. **Secondary:** reset understanding, ask‑why.
- **Belongs:** every live belief, fully legible. **Never:** a hidden belief; a belief without provenance.
- **Expectation:** nothing hidden; everything correctable. **Failure:** n/a. **Empty:** "LOCA doesn't believe much about you yet — that's honest."
- **Connections:** ← any corrected claim; → the evidence behind each belief.
- *(Sibling You/Trust surfaces — Privacy & Consent, Your Data, How‑it‑works, Settings — hold, respectively: the per‑source consent controls + payoffs; export/delete of everything; plain honesty about limits/reasoning; app preferences. Each is minimal, honest, and action‑bearing.)*

### DETAIL DESTINATIONS (contextual)
*Chapter · Event · Person · Observation · Saved answer* — each shows **the thing + its evidence/provenance + its actions** (correct, ask‑why, reflect, hide). Contract per each: purpose = show one entity honestly; belongs = the entity + lineage; never = an unexplained or unconfirmed assertion; evidence = its own; empty = n/a (reached only when it exists).

### SYSTEM · AMBIENT (contextual)
- **Onboarding:** L1 = "what LOCA is + one action"; content = one intent question, one first log, one seeded reflection, honest expectations. Never = a tour, a permission gauntlet, an overpromise.
- **Notifications:** content = the one weekly Review invitation; optional neutral habit reminders. Never = guilt, streaks, FOMO.
- **Widgets (Home/Lock):** content = one calm glance — a reflect invitation or a quiet piece of the record. Never = metrics, alarming numbers.
- **Watch:** content = quick log / state check‑in. **Search/Spotlight:** content = retrieval of the person's own record (zero‑claim). **Share‑in:** content = attach a note/photo to today.

## Part III — The Card / Element Architecture

Every element type, with its seven attributes. This is the minimal catalogue; no element outside it may be invented without a canonical need (Design6).

| Element | Why it exists | Appears when | Disappears when | Evidence | Confidence | Updates | Enables | Permanent? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Reflection prompt** | seed the daily loop | daily, on Today | after use / next day | none (not a claim) | n/a | daily | write a reflection | temporary |
| **Reflection entry** | the person's meaning | on write | never | n/a (the person's) | n/a | never (immutable) | revisit, reflect | **permanent** (record) |
| **Record entry (fact)** | the truthful record | on capture | never (unless corrected/deleted) | it *is* evidence | certain | on correction only | revisit, correct | **permanent** |
| **Observation (hedged)** | honest self‑understanding | L4 evidence + min confidence | evidence ages/stops; corrected | L4 required | shown, calibrated | as evidence changes | ask‑why, correct, reject, hide | temporary (decays) |
| **Habit row** | capture status | while habit active | on archive | facts | certain | on log | log, open | permanent while active |
| **Timeline landmark** | continuity | on the person's confirmation | never (historical) | candidate → confirmed by person | n/a (confirmed fact) | rarely | reflect, correct | **permanent** |
| **Review** | the ritual look‑back | weekly/monthly, once data exists | never (kept as artifact) | facts + ≤ few hedged obs | shown on obs | per period | reflect, save, ask‑why | **permanent** (artifact) |
| **Person card** | relational continuity | consent + recurrence | salience → 0 (fades, not deleted) | recurrence, provenance | salience honest | as presence changes | view, confirm, correct, hide | record kept; salience provisional |
| **Direction statement** | the person's aim | on authoring | on the person's edit | user‑authored | n/a | user‑led | edit, reflect | kept (past kept) |
| **Answer** | self‑inquiry result | on asking | re‑derivable; saved persists | record + L1–L4 | shown | on ask | save, ask‑why, refine | only if saved |
| **Belief** | the live user‑model | on corroboration | decays/retires (history kept) | L4 | shown + history | as evidence changes | inspect, correct, remove | live provisional; history kept |
| **Nostalgia teaser** | rediscovery | when a real past moment is relevant | after glance | a real past record entry | n/a | occasional | open the moment | temporary |
| **Empty state** | honest teaching | when a surface has no data | when data exists | n/a | n/a | as data arrives | learn, take the next step | temporary |

**The element law:** if a proposed element is not in this catalogue and cannot be justified as the canonical home of a real need, *it does not exist.* Every V2 card (traits, narrative, the 12‑card grid, the narrated read) fails this test and is gone.

## Part IV — The Information Priority Framework

- **Immediate attention (L1):** the *one thing the surface is for* — the reflect prompt on Today, the log on Habits, the answer on Ask, the record on Timeline. Exactly one focal purpose. *(Design5 one focal point.)*
- **Below the fold / L2:** the supporting facts and the ≤ few hedged observations, with their provenance available.
- **After interaction / L3:** evidence, provenance, confidence history, and the person's agency controls (correct/reject/hide/reset).
- **Hidden until evidence exists:** every claim‑bearing element (observations, patterns, landmarks, people) — absent, replaced by honest "not enough yet," until earned.

**The priority law (the calm rule):** when a surface gains more to show, the new content flows *downward* into L2/L3; **L1 stays singular and calm.** A surface never gets busier at the top as it learns — it gets *deeper underneath.* *(Design5; Design2.)*

## Part V — The Cross‑Linking Model

Every link exists for a reason; the person is never lost, and every path preserves purpose and back‑navigation.

- **An observation →** its evidence → the raw facts (ask‑why chain) → and the correction control. *(Design3 traceability.)*
- **A pattern →** the habit it concerns (and back); a habit → its patterns + its Timeline presence.
- **A memory / moment →** its provenance → and the person's reflection on it.
- **A relationship →** the Timeline moments they appear in → and the Understanding they inform.
- **An event →** the moment it marks → and the chapter it belongs to.
- **A Review →** the Timeline period it covers → and the observations it surfaced (each to Understanding).
- **A belief →** its evidence + confidence history → and the correction control.
- **Any claim →** always, "ask why?" → its faithful explanation. *(Design4; Design3.)*

**The linking law:** every link goes *toward* more evidence, more context, or more agency — never toward a feed, an upsell, or a dead end. Following a link always deepens understanding or extends control. *(Design6; S3.)*

---

## Part VI — The Progressive Detail Framework

Universal three‑level reveal, applied to every surface:

- **Level 1 — instantly understandable:** the surface's single purpose, graspable at a glance, no thought required. *(Krug; Design5.)*
- **Level 2 — after curiosity:** the supporting facts, context, and (where earned) the few hedged observations with their confidence.
- **Level 3 — after deliberate exploration:** the full evidence, provenance, confidence history, raw facts, and agency controls.

**The reveal law:** nothing at L2/L3 is required to use the surface; nothing at L1 exceeds one focal purpose. Depth is always *available and never imposed.* *(Design5; Design1 L1/L2/L3.)*

## Part VII — Context Preservation

When the person moves between surfaces, their context follows so they never rebuild it:

| Context | Follows from → to | So that… |
|---|---|---|
| **Selected habit** | Habit detail → Understanding / Timeline | patterns and moments show *that* habit, not all |
| **Time period** | Timeline / Review → each other, → Understanding | a "week" stays the week across surfaces |
| **Question** | Ask → its answer → the evidence | the inquiry frames what's shown |
| **Goal / Direction** | Direction → Reviews / Today | reflection is framed by the person's aim |
| **Relationship** | Person detail → Timeline / Understanding | shows *that* person's moments and relevance |
| **Pattern / observation** | Understanding → Habit / evidence | ask‑why lands on *that* pattern's basis |
| **Life chapter** | Chapter detail → Timeline / Understanding | the period's baseline frames what's shown |

**The context law:** moving deeper carries the current focus with it; the person never re‑selects what they already chose, and never mentally rebuilds where they are. *(Design6 consistency; R5 recognition over recall.)*

## Part VIII — The Content Lifecycle Model

Per content type: **birth → updates → aging → expiration → archiving → rediscovery** (from Design3/S5, applied to what the person sees):

- **Facts & reflections:** born on capture; never update (immutable), never age, never expire; permanently the record; rediscovered via Timeline & nostalgia. *(P6.)*
- **Observations & beliefs:** born on corroboration; update as evidence changes; age and decay if unsupported; expire from the *live* view (retire); archived in belief/confidence history; rediscovered if the pattern recurs. *(Design3 decay; S5.)*
- **Reviews:** born weekly/monthly; permanent artifacts; rediscovered via Timeline.
- **Landmarks (chapters/events):** born on the person's confirmation; permanent; rediscovered via Timeline.
- **Answers:** born on asking; re‑derivable; permanent only if saved.
- **Person cards:** born on consent+recurrence; salience updates; fades with absence (record kept); rediscovered when the person returns to the person's life.

**What fades:** unsupported observations/beliefs and lapsed salience (from the *live* view). **What always remains:** the record, reflections, confirmed landmarks, reviews. **What returns during important moments:** nostalgia ("this time last year"), recurring patterns, and past reflections relevant to a similar present. **The lifecycle law:** *not every observation deserves permanent visibility — but every fact deserves permanent existence.* Live content is curated by evidence; the record is curated by nothing. *(S5; Design3.)*

## Part IX — Information Integrity (no duplication)

Every concept has **one owner, one canonical source, and as many read‑only summaries/links as needed** (Design6). Resolution rule when a concept could appear twice:

- **Owns** it (canonical, editable, the source of truth): per the Design6 ownership table (e.g. a *pattern* is owned by **Understanding**; a *habit's history* by **Habit detail**; a *review* by **Reviews**; the *record* by **Timeline**).
- **Summarizes** it (read‑only glance, deep‑links to the owner): e.g. Habit detail *summarizes* a pattern that Understanding owns; Today *summarizes* a fresh Review; Reviews *summarize* the period's facts.
- **Links** to it (a purposeful path to the owner): every summary is also a deep‑link to the canonical home.

**The integrity law:** a concept is *edited and reasoned about in exactly one place*; everywhere else is a faithful, linked reflection of that one place. Two editable copies of anything are a defect. *(Design3; Design6.)*

## Part X — The Surface Interaction Contract (the reusable template)

Every surface's contract (Part II) — and every future surface — is specified by this template, which later guides implementation:

```
CONTRACT
  Purpose            — the one thing this surface is for
  Questions answered — the user questions it resolves
  Content (L1/L2/L3) — instantly / on curiosity / on exploration
  Actions            — primary (few) · secondary (fewer)
  Belongs / Never    — what earns a place · what is forbidden
  Inputs / Outputs   — what it consumes · what it produces
  Dependencies       — what it needs to function
  Evidence required  — the minimum to show any claim (else absent/abstain)
  Confidence required— the minimum to assert (else hedge or hide)
  User expectation   — what the person should feel/expect
  Failure behavior   — what happens when data/inference fails (honest, never fabricated)
  Empty behavior     — teaching, honest, never broken
  Connections        — canonical links in/out
```

**The contract law:** no surface may be built whose content cannot be fully specified by this template *and* justified against S3 (does it help the person see, remember, or make honest sense of their life?). A surface that can't fill its contract honestly isn't ready to exist.

---

## The content architecture, in one statement

> **Every surface in LOCA holds exactly one calm focal purpose at first glance, with supporting facts a level beneath and evidence, provenance, and the person's controls a level below that — and nothing appears at all until it is honestly earned. Facts and reflections live forever; observations and beliefs appear only with sufficient evidence, carry their confidence and lineage, decay when unsupported, and can always be traced, questioned, corrected, or removed. Every concept is owned and edited in one place and merely reflected everywhere else; every link leads toward more evidence, more context, or more agency, never toward a feed or a dead end; and every surface answers the same honest questions — what is this, where did it come from, how sure is it, what can I do with it, and what's empty and why — so that as LOCA learns, its surfaces grow deeper underneath while staying calm and singular on top.**

This content architecture is the definitive specification of what every surface contains. Design8 onward may specify *how this content is structured and expressed in the interface* — but may not add content a contract doesn't justify, duplicate an owned concept, or violate the calm‑not‑dense, evidence‑gated, or one‑canonical‑home laws.
