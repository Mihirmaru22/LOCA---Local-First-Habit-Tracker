# The Central Question

> **"Does LOCA let me *see* my life, or does it just *show me data* about my life?"**

This is the question the whole product is a bet on. Everything in the roadmap —
the engine, the ontology, the inference, the graph — is worth its thousands of
hours only if the answer eventually becomes *"it lets me see."* If, after the
roadmap is complete, the honest answer is still *"it shows me data,"* then that
is the problem to solve — **not by adding more models, but by rethinking how the
existing intelligence is experienced.**

---

## How to use this document

- **Written at:** end of Phase 6 (Structure over Time complete: signals →
  states → events → chapters → traits → people → relationship graph, all wired
  and seeded).
- **Revisit at:** the end of Phase 8 (the Perspective Interaction) and again at
  the end of Phase 10 (Hardening / pre-ship Gate B).
- **The test to re-run each time:** open the app on real (or seeded) months of
  data, ask one real question, and watch your own face. The difference between
  *"huh, a chart"* and *"…oh"* is the entire verdict. No document can decide it;
  only that reaction can.

If Phase 8 lands the *"…oh,"* this document has served its purpose and can be
retired. If it doesn't, read the "Cycle 2 directive" at the bottom before
writing a single new model.

## The verdict at Phase 6

**LOCA has built the entire telescope's optics and mount — and pointed them at a
wall of readouts about the user, instead of through the eyepiece at their life.**
The engine is real. The instrument is not there yet. This is the *predicted*
state at end of Phase 6 (the plan puts the substrate in Phases 2–6 and the
experience in Phases 7–8) — but it also surfaces one real inversion, noted
below.

## The seven findings

### 1. What is still missing before it feels "alive"
Everything the vision calls *the product*. The real surface is **a Present you
arrive at, a Reach gesture, Ask→View, Threads, Focus** — and none of the five
exist yet. Also absent: **Direction** (the self's aim; without it the manifesto
says we describe "a marionette, not the hand"), the **story voice** (we model the
trajectory but never speak the story), and any **daily aliveness** (the UI is
identical every open; the vision demands it be "truthfully different every day").

### 2. Is this exactly what was expected after Phase 6?
Mostly yes — the "I'm looking at internal models" feeling is the designed
midpoint. **But one thing genuinely failed to emerge:** the plan's own dominating
dependency — **Phase 1 / Gate A: prove by hand, with real people, that a view
feels like *seeing* not *reading*** — was never run. The expensive engine (2–6)
was built before the one interaction the whole product rides on was proven. That
is the exact inversion Phase 1 was front-loaded to prevent.

### 3. Capabilities that already exist but aren't surfacing
- Uncertainty is genuinely first-class (calibrated confidence on every
  state/trait; soft rendering exists) — but "soft where unsure" isn't the felt
  signature.
- A **counterfactual seed** already exists (`PersonalLifeViewUI` has a
  `counterfactualVariable` + toggle) — Victor's "bend it," present but inert.
- Cross-entity **insights** (`MultiEntityComposer` → `SceneInsight`) are computed
  but shown as a static list.
- **Regime-scoped chapter baselines** ("your normal, this chapter") — raw
  material for "seeing you made it out," unused for perspective.
- The **6.4 confounder rejection** ("this looked linked, but a life change
  explains it") — a novel trust move, hidden in a sub-list.
- A **voice channel** exists (`ReflectionDelivery`) but isn't the instrument's
  voice.

### 4. What potential is hidden from the user
The vision's flagship moment — *"Am I happier since starting my internship?"* as
a before/after you read yourself — **is fully computable today** (event, two
chapters, regime baselines, soft uncertainty all exist). The user can't feel it
because it's behind a form and drawn as charts. Same for "your mood tends to be
higher around Mom" and "two chapters rhyme."

### 5. Biggest critique opening it fresh today
**It reads as two stapled-together dashboards** — a habit card-grid, plus an
eye-icon browser of internal models (Chapters / People / Traits / Connections).
That Explore row is almost line-for-line the thing the vision's §9 *deletes*
("a card wall is a dashboard wearing a costume"). It makes the user **the analyst
of their own data** rather than the person being shown something. The manifesto
names this exact failure: *"a flat list of things to track — that is a database,
and it has no subject."*

### 6. What to expect at end of Phase 6 vs end of Phase 8
- **End of P6 (now):** *"The machinery works and it's impressive — but it's
  about me, at me. I'm reading reports on myself."* Interest, not recognition.
- **End of P8 (per plan):** arrive at a Present honestly different each day; ask
  one question and get a **scene you conclude from** (no chart, no verdict); pull
  a thread and your life becomes a walk; reach back and years dissolve into
  shape; correct something and feel it *take* it. Target: *"…oh. This is looking
  at me, now,"* and at least once the still moment ("I forgot that" / "I made it
  out").
- **Caveat the vision insists on:** even P8 only *earns* "genuinely new" if the
  view feels like seeing (Kay) **and** at least one view can be *bent* (Victor).
  Otherwise it is "a gorgeous, better-behaved analytics app."

### 7. Does the current experience match the intent of the frozen vision?
**No — and it currently contradicts three load-bearing intents**, which is why it
feels off rather than merely sparse:
- **Instrument vs. observer.** The vision is a telescope that extends sight then
  gets out of the way; what's built is a UI that *displays the system's findings*.
- **A self with no aim.** Built the self's weather (States/Traits) and world
  (People/Chapters), not its **Direction**. The marionette, not the hand.
- **The refusals — partial.** "Never a black box" is honored *well* (the most
  faithful part). But the deeper intent — *instrument, not report* — is violated
  by the browsing surface.

**What genuinely matches, and shouldn't be undersold:** the substrate is faithful
to the ontology; uncertainty is real and calibrated; fully on-device with no
second master; the 6.4 confounder-rejection is authentic epistemic honesty most
products never attempt. **The bones are right. The eyepiece isn't there yet.**

## The Cycle 2 directive

If, after Phase 8 (and confirmed at Phase 10), the honest answer to the central
question is still *"it shows me data"*:

**The problem is not a missing model. It is that the existing intelligence is
being displayed rather than experienced.** The fix is not more inference, more
entities, or more accuracy. It is the interaction grammar: the Present, the
Reach, Ask→View, Threads, Focus — and the proof (Kay's and Victor's) that a
single composed view feels like *seeing*, and that at least one view can be
*bent*, not just read.

Do not answer a felt-experience problem with an engineering answer. When this
document is reopened, resist the reflex to build. Re-run the face test first.
